An applied language recovery is an intervention, not proof of recovery success.

success after them separately. The latter is temporal evidence, not a causal
counterfactual or proof that the specific failed subtask was repaired.

payloads or credentials. Unique sensor evidence remains remote pending verified
full backup; these small reports do not authorize retirement or shutdown.
